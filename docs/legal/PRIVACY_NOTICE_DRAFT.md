## 2. Information we may process

Depending on the feature used, the service may process:

- account identifiers and contact information supplied by a login provider, including whether email or phone contact details have been verified;
- seller application details such as seller type, legal name, storefront name, city, business registration number, policy-version acknowledgements and review outcome;
- marketplace content such as product descriptions, images/video, seller messages, bids, reviews, delivery and order information;
- payment and payout references supplied by a payment provider (Katondo should not collect or store raw card credentials);
- support requests, moderation/audit records, security events and technical information such as IP address, browser/device details and service logs, where collected by the product or its providers.

Katondo should not request identity-document images, biometric data, passwords, full card details, or other sensitive personal data through the seller application form unless a documented legal basis, necessity, provider, safeguards and retention schedule have been approved.
