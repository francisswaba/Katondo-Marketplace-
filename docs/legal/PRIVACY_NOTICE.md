# Katondo Privacy Notice

Version 2026-12-01. Effective 1 December 2026.

This notice explains what personal information Katondo collects when you use the Katondo website and app, why we collect it, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and the choices and rights you have.

Katondo is run by **Katondo.zm Marketplace**, PACRA registration number 320261074354, of No. 8 Elm Road, Woodlands, Lusaka, Zambia. We are registered with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner as a data controller, reference Q2332NZHEY-23PLMNBHJ367-9371K1.

For anything about your personal information, contact us at **lukwesaswaba@outlook.com**, or use the personal-data form under **Help** on Katondo (you don’t need an account for that form).

## Who can use Katondo

You must be **18 or over** to create a Katondo account. We don't knowingly collect personal information about anyone under 18. If you believe someone under 18 has an account, tell us and we will close it and delete their information.

## What we collect

**When you create an account or sign in.** Your account is managed by our sign-in provider, Microsoft Entra. We receive your name and email address, and any other details you choose to give when you sign up, such as phone number, city, country, street address or postal code. If you sign in with Google or Facebook, we receive your name and email address from them. We never see or store your password.

**When you apply to sell.** Your legal name, shop name, city and whether you sell as an individual or a business. Individuals also give their NRC number and phone number. Businesses also give their PACRA registration number. We use these to check who you are and keep the marketplace safe. We don't ask for photos of your ID.

**When you use the marketplace.** Listings you create, messages you send and receive, reviews you write and receive, help and complaint cases, account-recovery requests, your account settings, and your orders.

**Messages.** We check messages automatically for phone numbers, email addresses, links and requests to pay outside Katondo, and show a safety warning when we find them. Katondo staff read a conversation only if someone reports it, and every time they do, it is recorded.

**Optional activity.** Only if you turn it on in your account settings: which pages and features you use, so we can improve Katondo. This is kept for 30 days. We never store your full IP address; we keep a scrambled version that can't be turned back into the address.

**Security records.** Sign-ins, staff actions and changes to accounts, so we can investigate misuse and protect your account.

## Why we use it

- To create and run your account and let you sign in.
- To show your listings and shop, and connect buyers and sellers.
- To check sellers' identity and business details before they can sell.
- To deliver messages, reviews and orders, and to handle payments when you pay through Katondo.
- To answer help requests and complaints, recover accounts, and resolve disputes.
- To keep Katondo safe: detecting fraud, scams, prohibited items and abuse.
- To meet our legal obligations, such as tax and accounting records.
- To send you account and order messages, and marketing messages only if you choose to receive them.

We rely on your consent, which you give when you create your account and accept this notice, and on what is necessary to provide the service you asked for and to comply with the law. You can withdraw consent at any time by closing your account; this doesn't affect what we did before.

## Who we share it with

- **Other users**, only as needed: buyers see a seller's shop name and listings; the person you message sees your name and messages; reviews show the reviewer's first name. Buyer ratings are never public.
- **Service providers who run Katondo for us**: Microsoft (sign-in), Railway (our servers and database), Netlify (our website), and Google or Facebook if you choose to sign in with them. When we add payment, delivery or email providers, we will name them here.
- **Payment providers**, when you pay through Katondo. They process your payment details; Katondo doesn't store card numbers or mobile-money PINs.
- **Authorities**, when the law requires it, or to protect people from fraud or harm.

We don't sell your personal information.

## Where it is stored

Our database and servers are in the Netherlands (European Union). Sign-in information is held by Microsoft in Europe. Our website is delivered through servers around the world. Storing information outside Zambia is authorised by the Data Protection Commissioner, and our providers protect it with encryption and access controls.

## How long we keep it

- **Your account**: while it is open. If you close it, it is hidden straight away and you can restore it for 30 days. After that we remove your name, contact details, preferences, the text of messages you sent, and your listings. Your shop is renamed "Closed shop", and we delete your sign-in account.
- **Orders, payments and seller verification records**: for as long as Zambian tax and company law requires, without your contact details once your account is closed.
- **Help and complaint cases**: kept, without your contact details once your account is closed, so we can show how complaints were handled.
- **Optional activity records**: 30 days.

## Your rights

You can ask us to:

- tell you what information we hold about you and give you a copy;
- correct information that is wrong;
- delete your information, or stop or limit how we use it;
- stop using it for marketing.

Send requests through the personal-data form under **Help**, or to lukwesaswaba@outlook.com. We may need to confirm who you are first. We aim to respond within 30 days. Some information we must keep by law, and we'll tell you if that applies.

You can change most settings and close your account yourself from your account page.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the **Office of the Data Protection Commissioner** at www.dataprotection.gov.zm. We'd appreciate the chance to put things right first.

## Security

We use encryption, access controls, multi-factor sign-in for staff, and records of staff actions. If a security breach affects your personal information, we will tell the Data Protection Commissioner within 24 hours, as the law requires, and tell you if you are affected.

## Changes to this notice

When we change this notice, we'll publish the new version here with its date. If the change is important, we'll tell you when you next sign in and ask you to read it.
