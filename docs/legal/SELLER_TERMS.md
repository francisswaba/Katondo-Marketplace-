## 1. What Katondo does

Katondo is a marketplace. It lets you list items and connects you with buyers. You sell your items to the buyer; Katondo isn't the seller, except for items Katondo sells from its own stock, which are clearly marked.

## 2. Who can sell

- You must be **18 or over**.
- **Individuals** must give their NRC number and a phone number they can be reached on.
- **Businesses** must give their PACRA registration number, and the person applying must be allowed to act for the business.
- Your details must be true and kept up to date.

We check every application before you can sell. We may ask for more information, and we may decline an application. If we decline, we'll tell you, and you can ask for a second look through the help centre.

## 3. Your listings

- Describe items honestly and accurately, with real photos, the true condition and the full price.
- Only sell items you own or are allowed to sell.
- Don't list anything illegal in Zambia, stolen, counterfeit, dangerous, or that requires a licence you don't have. This includes weapons, drugs and medicines, wild animal products and adult content.
- We may remove listings that break these rules, and we record why.

## 4. Fees

Katondo charges **no fees** at the moment. Commission and listing fees will be introduced once onboarding is complete. Before any fee applies, we will give you at least 30 days' notice, show the fees clearly, and you can stop selling before they start.

## 5. Payments

- **Through Katondo**: when payment through Katondo is available, buyers pay at checkout and payments are processed by licensed payment providers. We will explain how and when you are paid before this starts.
- **Outside Katondo**: a buyer may choose to pay you directly, for example in cash or by mobile money. That arrangement is between you and the buyer. Katondo is not part of it, can't track, reverse or refund it, and, to the extent the law allows, isn't responsible if something goes wrong with it.
- You are responsible for your own taxes on what you sell.

## 6. Orders, delivery and returns

- Deliver items as described and on time, using the delivery method agreed with the buyer.
- If you offer your own delivery, tell buyers the cost before they buy.
- Deal with returns and complaints fairly and promptly. Nothing in these terms takes away a buyer's rights under Zambian consumer law.

## 7. Messages, reviews and safety

- Keep messages polite and about the sale. Don't harass anyone or share someone else's personal information.
- Don't ask buyers for passwords, PINs or card numbers.
- Buyers and sellers can review each other after an order. Don't offer anything in exchange for a review or try to manipulate reviews. You can reply once, publicly, to a review of your shop.
- Anyone can report a listing, shop, message or review. Our staff will look into it.

## 8. Complaints about Katondo

If you disagree with something we've done, such as removing a listing or suspending your shop, open a case under **Help** on Katondo. You can ask for one second look, which is handled by a different member of staff.

## 9. Suspension and closing

We may suspend or close a shop that breaks these terms, puts buyers at risk or breaks the law. Where we can, we'll warn you first and explain why. You can close your account at any time from your account page, once your open orders are finished.

## 10. Auctions and live selling

Auctions and live selling aren't available yet. Separate rules will apply when they are.

We'll give you at least 30 days' notice of important changes to these terms. These terms are governed by the laws of Zambia, and disputes are decided by the courts of Zambia.
