## 1. Marketplace role

Katondo provides a marketplace service through which approved independent individuals, registered businesses and Katondo itself may offer products. The final terms must clearly identify who is the seller of record for each transaction, which party contracts with the buyer, and Katondo's role in payments, delivery, consumer obligations and dispute handling. No term may remove a right that cannot lawfully be excluded.

## 2. Eligibility and accounts

Seller eligibility, legal capacity, age/guardian conditions, permitted locations and business-registration requirements are [to be confirmed by counsel]. Sellers must provide accurate information, protect account credentials, promptly update material changes, and use only accounts they are authorized to operate. Account authentication and seller identity checks are separate; an authenticated account is not a verified seller.

## 3. Application and verification

Applications may be reviewed, held for further information, or rejected according to published criteria and applicable law. The collection of any identity or business documents must use an approved verification provider and secure workflow; do not send identity documents through ordinary email or marketplace messages. Katondo must disclose any manual or automated decision process, review/appeal route and retention period before applications open.

## 4. Listings and prohibited/restricted products

Sellers are responsible for accurate descriptions, ownership/authority, condition, price, stock and lawful sale. The final terms must link the approved prohibited/restricted goods policy and specify moderation, takedown, evidence-preservation and appeal procedures. That policy and category controls are not yet approved; do not claim all lawful product types are allowed.

## 5. Fees, taxes and settlement

Commission, listing fees, taxes, payment-provider costs, settlement timing, refunds, chargebacks, payout holds and payout methods are [not selected]. No seller fee or payout promise may be shown until approved and configured. Katondo will not describe a payment hold as escrow or operate a stored-value wallet unless counsel and the relevant regulator confirm that model is permitted and the provider contract supports it.

## 6. Orders and delivery

Available delivery modes, service areas, fees, delivery estimates, proof of delivery, failed delivery, returns and responsibility for loss/damage must be displayed before an order is placed. Seller-arranged delivery is available only where explicitly enabled by Katondo and offered by the seller; the buyer must see applicable charges and terms before checkout. The platform-managed delivery provider and operating policy are not yet selected.

## 7. Cancellations, returns, complaints and disputes

Eligibility windows, statutory consumer rights, evidence rules, response deadlines, refund authority, payout holds, escalation and appeal rights are [to be determined with counsel and operations]. A final policy must not promise a resolution time or remedy that Katondo cannot deliver.

## 8. Reviews, communication and safety

Rules for reviews, fraud, harassment, intellectual property complaints, product safety, moderation and account restrictions must be linked and operationally supported. Katondo may restrict unsafe or unlawful content under a clearly described process, subject to applicable law and appeal rights.

## 9. Auctions and livestreams

Auction eligibility, binding-bid rules, reserve price, bid increments, late-bid extension, winner payment deadline, failed payment, seller bidding, stream interruption, records and dispute outcomes are [to be approved]. Do not enable timed or live auctions until specific rules, provider capabilities, moderation and consumer disclosures are approved.

## 10. Data and intellectual property

The approved privacy notice governs personal-data processing. Sellers grant only the limited rights necessary to display and promote authorized listing content; the final scope, duration, sublicensing and removal process require legal review. Sellers may not upload personal data or content they lack authority to use.

## 11. Suspension, termination and changes

The final terms must specify valid grounds, urgent safety exceptions, notice, appeal, effects on open orders/payouts, data retention and account closure. Material changes require a lawful notice and acceptance process where required.

## 12. Liability and dispute resolution

All warranties, indemnities, liability limits, complaint channels, governing law and dispute-resolution clauses require counsel's review. Do not insert broad exclusions that conflict with consumer-protection or other mandatory law.
